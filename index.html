FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is RavenShield really free?
Yes. Every tool is free with no limits. Just create your free account. Because everything runs in your browser, there are no server costs to pass on to you.
Where is my data stored?
In your browser's local storage on your own device. Nothing is uploaded. The only network call any tool makes is the optional breach check in Password Forge, which uses k-anonymity: just the first 5 characters of a SHA-1 hash are sent, never your password.
How do decoys alert me without a server?
RavenVault uses plus-addressed tripwire emails (like you+rs-k39x@gmail.com) that land in your own inbox, so any mail to that alias proves the decoy leaked. You can also paste any dump, log or AI output into the Leak Matcher and it will find every decoy token you've planted, then seal the hit as evidence.
Can I verify evidence someone else exported?
Yes. Open RavenAudit → Verify and drop in a RavenShield evidence JSON. The format is documented, so anyone can re-verify the chain with a few lines of code.
Can I use the prompt-injection firewall in my own app?
Yes. The engine is a standalone JavaScript file (assets/js/lib/shield-engine.js) with no dependencies. See the docs.
Is it legal to use?
Deception tools are defensive. Use them only on systems, accounts and data you own or are authorized to protect.
