RavenRAG

Two detection channels

Catch AI knowledge bases and chatbots that leak your documents. RavenRAG hides an invisible fingerprint in the text and plants a trap fact, a believable but fictitious detail that exists nowhere else. If either one shows up in a model's answer, you know exactly which document leaked.

Make it plausible but fictitious. Never use a real fact. It must not exist anywhere else.

Paste a chatbot answer, a scraped page or any suspicious text. RavenRAG decodes hidden fingerprints and searches for every trap fact you've planted, including partial and reworded matches.

Trap registry

How the two channels work

Invisible fingerprint. A 32-bit ID is encoded as zero-width characters (U+200B / U+200C) and repeated through the text. Copy-paste and most indexers keep them. They survive verbatim leaks, but sanitizers and paraphrasing remove them.

Trap fact. A unique fictitious statement. LLMs paraphrase, but they keep rare proper nouns and numbers, so even a reworded answer gives the trap term away. Use both channels for the best coverage.

Tip: give each recipient their own watermarked copy and you'll know who leaked it.
